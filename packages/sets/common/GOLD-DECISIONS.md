# Gold cards — decisions settled in review (2026-10-15)

Why each gold card in DESIGN.md reads the way it does: the decisions the review rounds settled, with their reasons. A future review should not reopen these unless a card is genuinely broken.

## Esgrima × element
- Every card must show the SWORD in its image (a card that's pure bending fails). 
- Titles already rejected for root clashes in the same hand or across the gold tables: anything with "Estocada" (Estocada fantasma), "Tall/talls" (Tall rere tall), "d'acer" (Retruc/Bardissa d'acer), "Guàrdia" (Guàrdia camaleó), "Punt/Punta" (Punt feble), "llamp" (Llamp de sang freda), "Fulla" more than once, "Espasa" more than once, "gel/glaç" (water's Gel del mestre, Presó de gel; Xi's Venes; Acrobàcia's cards), "granit", "llosa", "flama", "buf" (Bufetada), "voladora" (Esmaixada al vol / Vol propulsat), "Bosc" (Bosc de tornados), "Toc".
- Flama a l'altra mà / Revés de foc pays when the sword is BLOCKED (the off-hand fire); "Punta de llamp"-style follow-up damage was a duplicate and became the lightning lock (Sabre de tro).
- Capa buida is the best card; keep it as is.
- Marioneta escarlata (blood puppet) is kept; the duplicate puppet in Artífex×Aigua is the user's call.
- Cauteritzar la ferida vs Xi's Cauteritzar: the user's call (parked).
- The water 10 must beat Sang sota la lluna (Aigua 9); "ell mateix inclòs" makes a lone boss strike itself.
- Arrossegar a la punta (taunt) was replaced: it repeated Bufetada insolent.
- Round 3: Cimitarra serp is the Terra BEAST card (undefendable from below; plain vs a horde accepted — every horde fix duplicated Ensartar or a kit card).
- Foc climbs 2/4/5/7/10 with Sabre de tro as the 5+5 finisher (every pairing has a 10).
- Ratxa esmolada (five chosen cuts) vs Onada trinxadora (three random blows) is a deliberate contrast (the wind obeys, the wave churns). The "attacks on you fail" version copied Salt del meteor and Capa buida.
- Title roots now taken or out: "cara" (Fogonada a la cara), "contra" (Contracorrent), "vent"/"aire" (air kit titles), "huracà", "Remolí", "ceg"/"enlluern", "Parada" (jargon), "Pica" (not a sword).
- Fora d'abast uses "no et pot atacar" (not "actua l'últim" = Palmell d'aire).
- Round 4: weapon-named titles (Sabre de tro, Cimitarra soterrània) are fine — Esgrima is the sword art and the image beats literalism; Arma arrelada is generic on purpose.
- Cimitarra serp → Cimitarra soterrània, "No es pot defensar, tret que l'objectiu jugui una Defensa més ràpida" (so it no longer kills Estocada fantasma in the same hand).
- Ensartar la filera: no lone-target clause (it failed the scene test; Cimitarra soterrània is the beast card).
- Fogonada a la cara: random card AND random targets. Xocs ardents: "contra qui xoqui". Wording fixed on Revés de foc, Arma arrelada, Contracorrent, Fulla assedegada.
- Final round (2026-10-15): MUST 1 (Contracorrent's scene now shows the trade) · SHOULD 5 applied (Fogonada 'combatents a l'atzar'; Xocs ardents 'que toqui… atacant o defensant'; Sabre de tro locks only this turn; Sense mans breaks when you're wounded; Ratxa esmolada at most two cuts on one foe — keeps a lone boss alive, stays under Tall rere tall) · optional: Cimitarra speed tie.

## Mestre del xi × element

- Presa de terra (permanent "only dice" on every wounded enemy) is KEPT as is: the best-loved card; its scale and permanence earn it over Poder apagat.
- Lligam de dolor is the pairing's horde card; being weak vs the lone unagi is accepted (the other four Aigua cards cover the boss).
- At most one revive in the 20; Bocanada robada is it (any heal raises the fallen by rules.md §6, so heals say "aliat dret").
- Fer-lo estàtua / Pes de ploma outgrowing Bloqueig mutu (Xi 5) in a hybrid hand is a kit-level question for the user, not a card fix.

- Fondre el cansament must never remove fatigue levels ("only rest clears fatigue").
- Round 3: Agulla xiulant CUT (thin overlap; Aire needed a sum-2 entry — Buidar els pulmons). Corrent sanador CUT (heal-over-time with thin overlap; Aigua needed a 2nd Atac — Transvasar la força). Don't bring either back.
- Titles rejected: Carn de marbre / anything marble or stone (reads as armour), Cos segellat ("cos" = Desbloquejar el cos), Cops de ploma ("Cop" ×3 in an Xi hand; Pes ploma = featherweight already reads "hits like a feather"), Espurnes als nervis ("nervi"), Cop a l'alè ("Cop", "alè" = Alè de drac), Lligam de sang ("sang" = Sang sota la lluna).
- Hivernació is a Defensa with no roll (like Ganxo salvavides) — a dice-stage note.
- Lligam de dolor (spread one wound across a horde, sum 8) and Venes de vidre (block-and-shatter finisher, sum 10) are different shapes.
- Bocanada robada is revive-only: the single revive in the 20.
- Round 4: Fondre el cansament cancels only the fatigue held when played (else it switches off the game's only brake).
- Efecte negatiu = only what an enemy card left (rules.md); Hivernació's lost turn is a price, not harm (ruling under the table).
- Entumit set again keeps the later end; Espina clavada depends on it (ruling under the table).
- Lligam de dolor's own blow doesn't trigger the link ("Després,"). Venes de vidre is a window (every wound doubled until end of next turn), above Lligam and apart from Xeringa de gebre.
- Cantar els punts is 1+3. Espurnes a les juntures pays 1 PV (Escut roent owns 2).
- Presa de terra says "es descarreguen a terra" (pun explicit). Verí rebotat and Raig redirigit titles kept (round 3 called them acceptable). Incendi interior numbs the wounded (its chi half).
- Round 5: Pell segellada → Segell de fang (earth shown). Bocanada robada → Boca a boca robat (revive reads from the title; single revive). Rejected: Bufada de vida ("vida" ×2 with Cop de vida; "Bufada" near Bufetada).
- Fer-lo estàtua and Raig redirigit now use the reviewer's exact wording (rules unchanged). Lligam de dolor: "els altres que has ferit". Asfíxia's scene puts the sealed breath points first.
- Round 6: Fondre el cansament cancels only the fatigue held at the START of the combat (else Incendi at max then Fondre makes the bid free). Incendi interior → Fornal humana (the master burns his own fuel; avoids "cos"). Pes de ploma → Força de ploma (its strength becomes a feather's). Rulings: a forced lost turn counts as a turn without attacking (Espina); a harm passed to an enemy that can't act on it is lost (Verí rebotat).
- Final round (2026-10-15): MUST 1 (Fornal humana bid capped at Esgotat) · SHOULD 2 (Fer-lo estàtua's window starts next turn; Asfíxia recast continues the count) — applied, plus rulings (pending lost action is harm; Lligam passes the actual loss).
- Confirm round: Fals cadàver protects only if you play an Atac next turn (replaying it chained immunity and made Asfíxia unanswerable); Hivernació is ally-only (self-chaining every other turn).
- Confirm 2: MUST 0 · SHOULD 2 applied as rulings (Raig's bolt counts as an attack; Fornal humana unplayable at Exhaust or worse). CONVERGED.

## Acrobàcia × element
- "A terra" is the acrobat's own state; in this set only Acrobàcia floors anyone, so each pairing floors its own targets (setup cards) and pays them off.
- Molinet de foc is a mass DEFENSA (was an Atac that copied Capbussó suïcida). Don't turn it back into an every-enemy Atac.
- Aliat projectil: the ally is lobbed and lands at round's end (untouchable while airborne). Not "es resol ara" (that copied Trampolí de mans); "meteor" belongs to Salt del meteor.
- Pilar ressort's cost (you land on the ground) keeps it under Esgrima×Terra's Espasa serp (undefendable at sum 8); a conditional or bouncing version copied other cards.
- Volta de flama is a Focus (first blow misses + burn): "…i queda a terra" would beat Esquiva i traveta; the margin version copied Cop desviat.
- Titles already rejected for clashes: Escut viu (Artífex card), Escut de cos (reads as body-shielding an ally), Roda de molí (molí/Molinet), Glaçar els caiguts (caigut root), Torxa en picat (a dive like Capbussó), Lliscada entre cames (assumes legs), Pista de gel (gel ×3).
- Bola de bitlles (the downed ENEMY is the ball) and Pilota humana (YOU are the ball) are both kept; the scenes say which.
- Llit de caliu keeps its title (passed every review; "caliu" also appears in other skills' gold cards — a cross-skill note for the user).
- Round 3: "caigut/caiguda" means knocked OUT in this game — never for a floored enemy (rules out Enterrar el caigut, Glaçar els caiguts).
- Pirueta glaçada keeps "glaç" (its only echo, Pica de glaç, needs Esgrima — three-skill hero only, like the llosa case).
- Corró esclafador replaced Roda de pedra; rejected: Roda esclafadora, Rodes de foc (a second fire wheel beside Molinet de foc).
- Catifa de foc (whole field, at once) and Llit de caliu (one body, over time) are the same fire payoff at two scales on purpose. Rejected: Terra cremada (reads as Earth), Flamarada rasant (flama root), Passada de torxa (scene unclear).

- Molinet de torxes replaced Molinet de foc (two "de foc" in Foc); rejected: guspires (Braçal de guspires), encès, roent, brases, flames, ardent, incendi.
